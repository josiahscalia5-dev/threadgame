Step 1 · Before the pull

Touch the glowing loop

Purl stops on the safe approach. Touch the gold yarn loop holding the carpet closed.

What Purl does
He reaches for the yarn and takes hold. The camera stays behind him, looking toward the upper ribbon.
What you see
The coral carpet is rolled ahead of Purl. Its gold fastening glows, and the cue changes from “Touch the loop” to “Pull down.”

This first lesson has no timer or penalty. Both racers’ race progress pauses while you learn. Opening the carpet awards no thread; Purl remains at 0/3 until he reaches the coral spool.
