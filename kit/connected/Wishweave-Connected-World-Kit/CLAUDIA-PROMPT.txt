CONTROLS AND MOVEMENT

Swipe left/right changes lateral position smoothly inside the walkable width; near a junction it selects the announced branch. A swipe must never teleport Purl onto an unrelated route or off an edge. At a bend the path heading turns the character naturally. Swipe up triggers one jump with a visible launch, arc, contact shadow and landing. Double-tap toggles a brief controlled deceleration to stop, then resumes on the next double-tap. Loop keeps racing during this player brake. Global Pause freezes the entire simulation. If Purl is stationary, swipe up may jump in place; it must not also resume forward movement. Provide accessible Left/Right/Jump/Run-Stop buttons in addition to gestures.
