Simple controls, natural movement

NormallyPurl runs automatically at a moderately brisk pace.
Swipe left / rightSteer within the road; choose a branch at a junction.
Swipe upJump. One gesture, one action.
Double-tapStop or resume Purl. Loop keeps racing.
Pause buttonPause both racers and the world.

What makes the race feel right

  • Alternating foot plants and push-offs match actual distance traveled.
  • The body leans into turns; the camera follows smoothly behind.
  • Purl stops in an idle pose, without running in place.
  • Loop has independent movement, decisions and reactions.
  • If Purl slows down, Loop can pass. Results are not forced.
  • The next area is visible before entry. The world never switches pages.

First playable proof

Check the movement before expanding the whole level

  1. Import or adapt the route and build the opening rug, tower bend and entry into the next area.
  2. Demonstrate steering, a grounded jump, stopping while Loop passes, and resuming—all in an uninterrupted run.
  3. Show the playable build and recording from the same source version. Verify foot contact, camera continuity, depth and Android screen fit.
  4. After that foundation is accepted, extend through all six connected areas and finish the three pickups, Snagling, butterfly and race results.
