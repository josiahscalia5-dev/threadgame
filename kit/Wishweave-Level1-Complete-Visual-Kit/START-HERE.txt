WHAT YOU APPROVED
01-ribbon: Tap a stitch to unfurl the first coral ribbon; collect the coral thread.
02-shortcut: Tap a stitch to unroll a connected shortcut. This action does not award another thread.
03-tassels: Tap the stitch to raise three tassels and reach the teal thread.
04-snagged: One possible missed dodge; Snagling briefly tangles Purl and drops one held thread.
05-recovery: Recover the SAME dropped thread and resume. A successful dodge skips this branch.
06-butterfly: Tap the tapestry stitch; an embroidered butterfly brings the golden thread.
07-finish: Reach the flower gate with all three held threads; show the true race result.

Purl is the red player. Loop is the teal COMPUTER RIVAL. They compete; they do not rescue or tether to each other.
